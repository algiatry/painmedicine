## Notes for sending

- **Who first.** Fellows finishing pain fellowships and early-career
  attendings respond best to the credited-byline angle. Semi-retired pain
  physicians respond to the mission angle. Lead with the matching bullet.
- **Where.** ASRA, AAPM, and AAPM&R member directories; fellowship program
  pages (the `/how-pain-physicians-train` article already lists the six
  certifying boards); LinkedIn "medical reviewer" searches for clinicians who
  already do this for other sites.
- **Personalize the one bracketed sentence.** It is the only line that
  distinguishes this from a mass mailing, and it is the line that gets replies.
- **Attach nothing** on the first touch. Links only. The brief goes out on
  reply.
- **Have the letter of agreement ready** before you send, so "yes" can be
  followed the same day. The brief's boundaries section is the outline;
  have counsel look at the draft.
